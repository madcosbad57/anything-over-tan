About · The Manifesto

Any color you like — as long as it's over tan.

anythingovertan is a long-form blog about cars worth buying. We celebrate one specific kind of decision — the one where a thoughtful enthusiast picks a paint color, then quietly insists, against the dealer, against fashion, sometimes against their spouse, that the interior must be tan.

It is not about the leather. It is about what the leather tells you. A car ordered with a saddle interior was ordered by someone who planned to keep it. Someone who looked at the spec sheet, saw the boring black option that depreciates the slowest, and ordered cognac anyway. We are interested in those people, and we are interested in the cars they bought.

The Constant

Tan is the thread that ties twenty-five years of good taste together.

From a 993 Carrera 4S in Polar Silver, to an E39 Touring in Oxford Green, to a Defender 110 in Coniston, to a 996 Carrera in (yes) Guards Red — the cars we love most are connected by a single design choice. A warm interior. A reason to look down at the dashboard. Every story we publish leads with a "[paint] over Tan" spec line, because the spec is the story.

This is not nostalgia. There are tan-on-tan cars rolling out of factories in Stuttgart and Goodwood right now. The choice is, has always been, and will continue to be, available. We are simply here to argue, at length, that you should make it.

The Masthead

Six people, one editorial line.

We are a small, fully-distributed team of writers and photographers. Every story is reported, written, photographed, and driven by one of us. We do not run sponsored content, and we do not take press cars longer than 72 hours.
